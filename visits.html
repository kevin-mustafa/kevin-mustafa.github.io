How visits to this site are logged

This site keeps a log of visits so I can see whether, and how, the people reading my job applications use it. This page sets out everything the log records, when your browser sends it, how long it is kept, and how to opt out or ask for your records.

Who keeps the log

Kevin Mustafa, in Brisbane, Australia. Contact: kevin.mustafa.dev@gmail.com.

When your browser sends a message

A short script on every page, assets/visits.js, sends a small plain-text message to the log service:

  • when a page opens;
  • every 15 seconds while the page is on screen and you are using it;
  • when you click a link or a download (a tracked click);
  • when the page is hidden or closed.

The messages carry no cookies. With scripts turned off in your browser, nothing is sent at all.

The log is kept until I delete it; you can ask me to delete your visits at any time (see below). Cloudflare keeps a restore copy of the database for 7 days, so a deletion is complete 7 days later.

Visitors in Europe, and privacy signals

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are logged the same way as everyone else. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, the log notes it, but it does not change what is logged; the switch below stops the log in this browser.

Why

To see whether, and how, the people reading my job applications use this site; to improve the site; and to keep it working and spot misuse. Where European rules apply, the basis is my legitimate interest in understanding how my application material is used, kept in check by the limits on this page.

Who else handles it

GitHub hosts these pages (GitHub Pages); the visit log gets nothing from GitHub. Cloudflare runs the small program that receives the messages and stores the log in its database. Cloudflare reports the database's region as Oceania (OC). To name a business network, the log service asks the public internet registry (APNIC, or rdap.org, which passes the question to the right registry) about the network block, never your full address, and asks Cloudflare's public DNS service for the address's reverse DNS name. Only I can see the log, on a private page that needs my sign-in.

What it is never used for

The log is never sold, shared or published, apart from the visit count on the home page, and never used to contact anyone about a visit. I do not try to find out who you are beyond the application the link belongs to. There are no cookies, ads or outside trackers, and the script takes no fingerprint of your device (no canvas, graphics, audio or font readings).

Opt out

Turn the switch on to stop the visit log in this browser. It keeps one setting in your browser's storage for this site (not a cookie, and never sent anywhere) that stops the script on every page. When you turn it on, one message with no details is sent, so I can count opt-outs.

The switch needs scripts turned on.

See or delete your records

Email me at kevin.mustafa.dev@gmail.com. Give the date, the rough time and the pages you opened (or the link you used), and I will send you what the log holds about that visit, correct it or delete it. You can ask me to delete your visits at any time, and you can object to the log at any time; the switch above does that for this browser.

Complaints

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland and are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to your data protection regulator: your country's supervisory authority in the EEA, the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, or the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner in Switzerland.
