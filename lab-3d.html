Rack: switches, ports and shelves30 items

Open-frame rack

  • The two managed switches and three desktop servers sit in an open-frame rack.
  • My records confirm what is in the rack, but not the exact shelf or unit order, so the drawing shows the switch fronts port by port and the server shelves without naming which machine is where.
  • The router and the small 2.5 gigabit switch are not drawn in the rack because their placement is not recorded.
  • Every core switch port has a label typed on the switch naming the machine on the far end, so the switch's own screens match the cabling.

See: Rack and cabling sheet

Core switch (8 port 10 gigabit fibre)

  • Model: TP-Link TL-SX3008F, 8 SFP+ ports at 10 gigabit, managed.
  • Ports 1 to 6 and 8: servers and the training laptop over multimode fibre.
  • Ports 3 and 4: storage server, bundled as one 20 gigabit link (static link aggregation).
  • Port 7: 1 gigabit fibre trunk to the access switch (LACP, one member).
  • Jumbo frames: maximum frame size raised to 9216 bytes, the switch maximum.
  • Port labels typed on the switch: PC2_z690, PC4_z97, PC3_z490, PC3_z490, PC1_x870e, PC1_x870e, Trunk_SG2218, M5Max_Mac.
  • Firmware updated in October 2026 after a management freeze; the old image is kept as a fallback.

Router (home router, Wi-Fi 6)

  • Model: ASUS RT-AX86U Pro (Wi-Fi 6, one 2.5 gigabit port).

Storage server (twelve drives, RTX 3070)

  • 10 gigabit: Mellanox ConnectX-3 Pro dual-port card, both ports on core switch ports 3 and 4, bonded as one link (static aggregation on the switch, matching bond in TrueNAS).

  • Mapped all twelve drives by serial number before the storage pools were built, so no drive is ever identified by a name that can change between boots.
