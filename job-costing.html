Method and sources

The formulas, in plain words

  • Machine cost an hour = machine cost × (depreciation down to the resale value over the life + cost of money on the average amount tied up + upkeep) ÷ hours a year. At the defaults that is cost × 0.3088 a year ÷ 7,000 hours.
  • Machine cost of today's machines = what they would sell for (indicative resale, 20 Aug 2026), the cost of keeping rather than selling them; new parts at their price.
  • Electricity an hour = kW × cents a kWh × (1 + cooling).
  • Rent an hour = cards × US$ price × A$ per US$ × (1 + GST and extras); API prices the same way, per million tokens.
  • Cost per token = cost an hour ÷ tokens an hour.
  • Whole lab after a change = (today's cost an hour + the added cost an hour) ÷ (today's tokens an hour + the added tokens an hour).
  • Per word = per token ÷ 0.75 words per token.
  • Per parameter = 20 × the cost per token, for a compute optimal run (20 training tokens per parameter).
  • Kept tokens (training data) = tokens written × 86 percent kept after checks.

Kinds of number

  • Measured measured on the lab by a meter, sensor or log
  • My record my own record: a bill, invoice or order
  • Quoted a seller's price on a date
  • Published a published figure: a benchmark, price list or paper
  • Estimate an estimate: an assumption, said so
  • Computed computed from the other figures
